This Privacy Policy explains how Piegeon ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Searious Battles mobile application (the "App"), and what choices and rights you have. It applies only to the App; our Terms of Service are a separate document.

1. Information We Collect

Age Verification

The first time you open the App, you're asked to enter your date of birth (month, day, year) via a neutral screen — the same screen, with the same wording, for every player. Nothing on this screen hints that any particular answer unlocks anything.

Your birth date is used once, locally on your device, to calculate whether you're under 13. The birth date itself is never saved anywhere — only the result of that calculation (a true/false flag) is stored locally on your device, together with a flag noting that the screen has been completed. Returning players aren't asked again. If this check can't be completed for any reason, the App defaults to treating the session as a child's for ad purposes — it never assumes you're older than 13 by default.

This flag determines which ad experience you see, as described in Section 4 (Children's Privacy). No ads of any kind are requested or shown until this screen has been completed.

Consent for Advertising (EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states)

Where the law requires it, the App shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) after the age screen and before any ads are requested. There you can accept, reject, or manage how our ad partners may use your data. You can change your choices at any time with the "Privacy options" button in the App's Settings menu; this button is shown wherever such a choice is legally required.

Advertising & Diagnostic Data

The App shows banner ads on its menu screens, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. Rewarded ads are always optional: they only play when you tap a button that offers a reward, and skipping one simply means no reward. Ads are delivered through Google AdMob mediation. For each ad, AdMob runs a real-time auction (bidding) among the following ad networks:

  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
  • ironSource Ads (part of Unity)
  • InMobi
  • Liftoff Monetize (formerly Vungle)
  • DT Exchange (Digital Turbine)
  • Mintegral — only for players 13 and over; it never receives ad requests from child sessions (see Section 4)

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases: "Remove Ads" (removes banner and full-screen ads; optional rewarded ads stay available if you want them); in-game gold in three sizes — "Gold Pouch" (2,000 gold), "Gold Chest" (7,500 gold) and "Treasure Hoard" (16,000 gold); and the "Boom Crate" (6 rockets + 6 grenades), which are one-time-use power-ups. All payments are handled by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details (such as card numbers) — Google processes the payment and tells the App only which item was bought, so it can be delivered. The App stores the result locally on your device (whether ads were removed, your gold balance, and how many power-ups you have) and, if you use cloud save, as part of your save file (see Section 2). Purchases are subject to Google Payments' privacy notice. Parents can require a password or other authentication for purchases in the Google Play Store settings.

Game Progress & Cloud Save

On Android, the App uses Google Play Games Services to let you sign in with your Google account and to sync your game progress (level completion, stars, power-ups, purchases, settings) across devices. If you aren't signed in, or while offline, progress is saved locally on your device using standard storage mechanisms. See Section 2 below for what this sign-in accesses.

Reviews & Updates

The App may occasionally show Google Play's built-in "rate this app" window, and Google Play's in-app update prompt when a new version is available. Both are provided and run entirely by Google Play. The App does not learn whether you left a review or what you wrote, no data from these windows is sent to us, and the App works the same whatever you choose.

Information We Don't Collect

We do not require account creation to play the App (Google sign-in for cloud save is optional — see Section 2). The App does not collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying information beyond what Google Play Games Services provides for sign-in, and beyond what Google Play Billing requires to process purchases (which we never see directly).

This is optional — you can play the App fully without signing in, in which case no Google Account data is accessed and progress is saved locally only. If a future update adds new Google API scopes (for example, leaderboards or achievements), we will update this section and, where required, prompt you for consent before the change takes effect.

3. How We Use Information & Legal Basis

Any data described above is used solely to display and measure advertising, to deliver in-app purchases, to save and sync your game progress and to diagnose and fix technical issues. We do not sell your personal information for money. For how ad-related data counts under US state laws, see Section 8.

Where the GDPR applies (EEA/UK users), we and our ad partners rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for personalized/targeted advertising and any non-essential tracking identifiers. Advertising consent is collected through the consent message described in Section 1 and applies only to players 13 and over. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Section 7).
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — for delivering in-app purchases you make and for cloud save if you choose to sign in.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for non-personalized (contextual) advertising needed to keep the App free, and for crash/diagnostic logs needed to keep the App functioning correctly.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — for the age screen described in Section 1, which exists to comply with COPPA and Google Play's Families policy. This processing isn't optional or consent-based, since it's how we determine and apply the legally required child-safe ad experience.

The App does not use any data described above to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. Ad selection may involve automated, algorithm-based targeting by our ad partners, but this does not amount to profiling with legal effect.

4. Children's Privacy

Searious Battles has a mixed audience: it's designed to be enjoyed by both children under 13 and older players. Because of this, the App uses a neutral age screen (described in Section 1) to determine which ad experience applies to each player, in line with Google Play's Families policy and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • If you're under 13 (or if age couldn't be determined): the App tags every ad request as child-directed and limits ads to content rated for general audiences (G). This disables personalized advertising, remarketing, and interest-based ads, and prevents your device's advertising ID from being used for advertising. This signal is set through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), which passes it to every mediated ad network. Child sessions only use ad units whose auctions include Google Play Families Self-Certified ad networks (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, ironSource, InMobi, Liftoff Monetize and DT Exchange); Mintegral, which is not self-certified, never receives ad requests from child sessions.
  • If you're 13 or over: standard (non-child-directed) ads may be shown, as described in Section 1.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond the minimal, non-identifying data described in Section 1 (device/advertising identifiers are never collected for child sessions; diagnostic and crash data is not personal or directly identifying). In-app purchases always go through Google Play, where parents can require authentication for every purchase. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information beyond what's described here, please contact us using the details in Section 11 and we will delete it.

5. Data Retention

We do not maintain a personal user data profile on our own servers — account-linked data (Play Games profile and cloud save) lives on Google's infrastructure, as described in Section 2, and purchase records live with Google Play Billing. The only data related to the age screen that's ever stored is two true/false flags saved locally on your device (whether the screen was completed, and whether the session is treated as a child's); your actual birth date is discarded immediately after that calculation and is never transmitted anywhere. Your advertising consent choices (where applicable), your purchases (whether ads were removed, your gold balance, and how many power-ups you have) and your game progress are also stored locally on your device. Advertising identifiers, app interaction data, and diagnostic data are retained by our ad partners listed in Section 1 according to their own retention policies. Local data stored on your device, including the age-screen flags and consent choices, can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data via your device settings — doing so will show the age screen again on next launch. Purchases are tied to your Google Play account by Google and can be restored by Google Play after reinstalling.

6. Data Security

All data collected by the App and its third-party SDKs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (TLS). We take reasonable measures to protect the App and the limited data it processes, but no method of electronic processing or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Nie zbieramy świadomie danych osobowych dzieci poniżej 13 lat poza minimalnymi, nieidentyfikującymi danymi opisanymi w Rozdziale 1 (identyfikatory urządzenia/reklamowe nigdy nie są zbierane w sesjach dzieci; dane diagnostyczne i dzienniki awarii nie są danymi osobowymi ani nie identyfikują bezpośrednio).
